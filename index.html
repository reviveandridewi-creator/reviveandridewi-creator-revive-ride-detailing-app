Technical & Safety Manuals

Express Exterior Wash 35-45 mins

  1. Setup & Wheels (10 mins): Hook up Active 2.0 pressure washer. Spray wheel cleaner/tire degreaser. Scrub tires, barrels, and wheel faces. Pressure rinse.
  2. Pre-Foam & Agitation (7 mins): Foam vehicle with Active 2.0 foam cannon. Detail badging, door handles, and front grilles while foam dwells.
  3. Pressure Rinse & Contact Wash (12 mins): Rinse foam top-to-bottom. Perform a 1-bucket or multi-mitt contact wash working top-to-bottom. Rinse.
  4. Dry & Protect (10 mins): Apply spray wax as a drying aid while wet. Blow out mirrors, jambs, and wheels. Final wipe and apply tire shine.
Efficiency Hack: Never dry a naked car. Always spray your spray-wax/sealant while wet before using your drying towel.

Essential Interior Reset 45-60 mins

  1. Trash, Mats & Air Blowout (10 mins): Remove trash and floor mats. Use compressed air/Tornador to blast out vents, dash cracks, seams, and seat tracks down to carpet.
  2. Vacuum (15 mins): High-powered vacuum pass over seats, carpets, and trunk space.
  3. Surface Wipe & Sanitize (15 mins): Spray interior cleaner onto microfiber towel. Wipe dash, steering wheel, console, cup holders, and door panels.
  4. Glass & Rubber Mats (10 mins): Pressure-wash and dry rubber floor mats. Clean interior glass using the two-towel method.
Two-Towel Glass Method: Keep one towel slightly damp with glass cleaner to scrub, and a bone-dry waffle-weave towel to immediately buff off.

Steam Sanitization & Deep Interior 1.5 - 2.0 hours

  1. Trash & High-Pressure Blowout (15 mins): Clear mats/trash. Blast crevices, seat tracks, and vents with air while warming steamer.
  2. Dry Vacuum Pass (15 mins): Vacuum loose heavy dirt before applying steam or liquid.
  3. Steam & Agitation Phase (45 mins): Steam vents, tracks, leather, and door cards. Spray spot cleaner on fabric stains, agitate with drill brush, and steam extract/wipe clean.
  4. Final Vacuum, Protectant & Glass (20 mins): Quick vacuum pass for loosened debris. Protect plastic/vinyl with interior dressing. Wipe glass.

The 'Wet-Hold' Full Detail 1.5 - 2.5 hours

  1. Exterior Wash (25 mins): Clean wheels/tires. Foam, brush crevices, contact wash, heavy flood rinse. STOP HERE - Leave exterior soaking wet.
  2. Interior Blowout (10 mins): Aggressively blast vents, seat tracks, and crevices with air. Flying dust lands on wet paint and sticks to water droplets.
  3. Interior Deep Clean (35 mins): Vacuum carpets/seats, scrub dash/panels, apply UV protectant, clean interior windows.
  4. Exterior Re-Rinse (5 mins): Fast top-to-bottom pressure rinse to blast away settled interior dust.
  5. Exterior Dry & Protect (15 mins): Spray ceramic sealant/spray wax on wet panels as drying aid. Blow out water and dry.
  6. Final Touches (10 mins): Clean exterior glass, dress tires, apply trim restorer, re-install mats.
Dust Control Mastery: Blowing out an interior creates a dust cloud. Using wet exterior paint as a 'magnet' traps dust—just avoid direct sunlight to prevent water spots.

Paint Correction Services 2.5 - 6.0+ hours

  1. Decon Wash Phase (30 mins): Wheel clean -> Foam & Wash -> Apply Iron Remover (MWC neat) -> Clay mitt/bar pass.
  2. Drying & Prep Tape (20 mins): Blow dry paint completely. Mask off trim, emblems, rubber seals, and sensitive body lines with painter's tape.
  3. Correction Phase:
    • 1-Step (1-1.5 hrs): Machine polish to clear ~50-70% defects.
    • 2-Step (2.5-3.5 hrs): Heavy cutting compound pass followed by fine finishing polish.
    • 3-Step (4+ hrs): Heavy compound -> Medium refining -> Ultra-fine jewel polish.
  4. Panel Prep & Protection (25 mins): IPA/Panel Prep wipe down to remove polishing oils. Apply ceramic sealant or wax. Buff off and remove tape.

Campers & RV Exterior Wash 1.0 - 1.5 hours

  1. Roof Prep & Rinse First (20 mins): Start on roof or upper cap. Push dirt downward. Pressure rinse top-to-bottom.
  2. High-Volume Pre-Foam (15 mins): Foam high-up panels with long wand/extension nozzle.
  3. Extended Brush Contact Wash (30 mins): Use soft-bristle pole brush on a telescoping handle to scrub side panels without moving ladders.
  4. Final High-Volume Rinse & Air Blowout (20 mins): Top-to-bottom rinse, blow off glass panels, shine tires, and clean lower storage compartments.
Ladder Elimination: A 6-to-12ft pressure washer extension and telescoping brush eliminates 80% of ladder climbing.

Koch-Chemie Pol Star (Po) 1:5 to 1:20 (Water) Neutral cleaner for fine leather, Alcantara, carpets. Agitate with soft brush and wipe clean.
Koch-Chemie Gentle Snow Foam (GSF) 40-50ml in Foam Lance (~1:20 to 1:30) pH-neutral pre-wash snow foam. Allow foam blanket to dwell 3-5 mins before pressure rinsing.
P&S Brake Buster RTU or 1:1 (Maintenance) Non-acid wheel & tire cleaner. Spray on cool wheels, let dwell for 2 mins to melt brake dust before rinsing.
Turtle Wax Leather Mist Ready To Use (RTU) Mist onto microfiber applicator or surface. Gently agitate finished leather and buff dry.
Griot's Leather 3-in-1 Ready To Use (RTU) Work into leather with horsehair/detailing brush to speed up cleaning. Wipe away lifted grime.
Lexol Leather Conditioner Ready To Use (RTU) Apply moderate amount with foam pad to genuine leather, allow 5-10 mins to penetrate, buff off excess.
Meguiar's Last Touch Detailer RTU (Quick Detail) / 1:1 (Clay Lube) Final paint wipe down, gloss enhancement, or diluted 1:1 with water as a clay bar lubricant.
Meguiar's Hyper Dressing (D170) 1:1 (High Gloss) to 4:1 (Natural Satin) Water-based dressing for rubber tires, plastic engine trim, and vinyl moldings. Avoid floor controls.
Meguiar's Hybrid Ceramic Wax Ready To Use (RTU) Spray on wet panels after wash and rinse off thoroughly, or spray-on/wipe-off on dry paint.
Meguiar's Carpet Re-Fresher Ready To Use (RTU) Vacuum interior, mist lightly over carpets and cloth upholstery, allow to air dry completely.

Technical & Safety Manuals

Exterior Wash & Decontamination Exterior SOP

  1. Perform pre-rinse to remove heavy surface dirt and debris.
  2. Apply Wheel & Tire Cleaner; agitate with designated brushes.
  3. Foam vehicle with Ultra Foam Soap; allow 3–5 minutes dwelling time.
  4. Two-bucket contact wash starting from top to bottom.
  5. Rinse thoroughly and apply Iron Remover if paint decontamination is needed.
  6. Dry using microfibers and air blower for crevices.

Interior Deep Clean & Extraction Interior SOP

  1. Blow out all seams, vents, and under-seat rails using compressed air.
  2. Thoroughly vacuum carpets, mats, and upholstery.
  3. Clean plastics, vinyl, and leather using Interior All-Purpose Cleaner.
  4. Pre-treat fabric stains; agitate and extract with hot water extractor.
  5. Clean all glass streak-free using designated glass towels.

Heavy Duty Wheel Cleaner High Risk

Active Agent: Organic Acid Blend

PPE Required: Safety Goggles, Chemical Gloves, Mask

First Aid: Flush eyes/skin immediately with water for 15 mins. Seek medical attention if irritation persists.

All-Purpose Cleaner (APC) Moderate Risk

Active Agent: Alkaline Surfactant Base

PPE Required: Nitrile Gloves, Eye Protection

First Aid: Rinse skin/eyes with cold water. In case of ingestion, do not induce vomiting.

Ceramic Coating / Sealant Low-Mod Risk

Active Agent: SiO2 / Solvent Mixture

PPE Required: Respirator (Vapor), Nitrile Gloves

First Aid: Move to fresh air if inhaled. Flush eyes immediately.
